Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Cryptospec ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Summary: Cryptospec is designed to be privacy-first. All quantitative analysis runs 100% on your device. We do not collect personal data, trading history, or financial information.

Information We Collect

Data Processed On-Device

The following data is processed entirely on your device and never transmitted to our servers:

  • Cryptocurrency price data (fetched from CoinGecko API)
  • Macroeconomic indicators (fetched from FRED API)
  • Simulation parameters and results (Monte Carlo, regime detection, risk analysis, whale detection, backtesting)
  • Your app preferences (theme selection, configuration settings)

Analytics & Crash Reporting

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand app usage and fix bugs. These services may collect:

  • Anonymized usage patterns (which features you use, how often)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, screen size)
  • App performance metrics (load times, error rates)
  • Crash reports (stack traces, memory state at time of crash)

This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify individual users. You can opt out by disabling analytics in your device settings.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifier (advertising ID)
  • IP address (anonymized)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads you view or tap)

AdMob uses this data to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings or by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-Party APIs

Cryptospec fetches data from these third-party services:

  • CoinGecko API: Cryptocurrency market data. See CoinGecko Privacy Policy.
  • FRED API (Federal Reserve Economic Data): Macroeconomic indicators. See FRED Terms of Use.

These services have their own privacy policies. We do not control how they handle your data.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We explicitly do not collect:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial account details or trading account credentials
  • Your actual trades or portfolio holdings
  • Location data
  • Contacts or SMS messages
  • Camera or microphone access

How We Use Your Information

The limited information we collect is used to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality
  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users

Data Security

All quantitative analysis runs on your device. Price and macro data is cached locally and not transmitted to our servers. We use industry-standard encryption for any data in transit.

Children's Privacy

Cryptospec is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Disable analytics tracking

To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
